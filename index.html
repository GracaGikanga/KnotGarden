Frequently Asked questions

Do you offer food and beverage facilities?

"No. We do not offer in-house eatery facilities."


Do you provide seating and funiture for events?

"No, however we can coordinate with external providers to provide seats and tables."


Do you provide outdoor tents or shade structures?

"Yes we offer quality tenting and tent set up services that can be included when booking."

Can clients bring their own food?

"Yes. Clients are allowed to bring their own cooked meals or snacks to their planned event."

Am I permitted to hire an outside caterer for my event

"Yes. Clients are allowed to source their own external food catering services. This should be communicated before the event date when booking; to coordinate schedules, setup requirements and cleanup protocols. ""

Is parking available for guests?

"Yes.We offer ample parking spaces for our clients."

Are there restroom facilities on-site?

"Yes, our venue is equipped with clean, modern, fully private restrooms on-site to comfortably accommodate your guests throughout your event."

What is the payment plan?

"To secure your booking, a 50% commitment deposit of the total rental fee is required upon signing the agreement. The remaining balance must be paid in full at least 7 days prior to your scheduled event date."
